Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

What is the medium of instruction?
We offer fully English Medium instruction at our Jawahar Nagar branch. (Hindi medium is available at our Vigyan Nagar branch).
What is the admission process?
Simply fill out the online admission enquiry form below. Our counselor will contact you within 24 hours to schedule a campus visit and guide you through the documentation process.
Do you provide transport facilities?
Yes, we provide safe and secure vehicle facilities covering major routes across Kota for the convenience of our students.
What is the fee structure?
Our fee structure is highly competitive and designed to make quality education accessible. For exact class-wise fees, please submit an enquiry or call our admission desk.
